About the company

Two decades of measured, dependable finance.

Incorporated on 18 May 2005 and headquartered in Mulund, Mumbai, Total Holdings & Finvest Private Limited has grown as a privately held NBFC serving investment and lending needs across India.

Our approach is deliberately conservative: we grow our book responsibly, hold ourselves to the standards of a regulated financial institution, and treat every rupee entrusted to us with care.

  • Registered with the Reserve Bank of India as an NBFC
  • Governed by an experienced, designated board of directors
